Research Areas

Spanning neuroscience, nanotechnology, and aquatic biology — exploring life across scales.

01
Neurophysiology
Circadian rhythms, serotonin dynamics, and suprachiasmatic nucleus function across developmental stages and aging in rat brain models.
02
Developmental Biology
Growth, differentiation, and molecular mechanisms underlying animal development including neurodevelopmental aspects.
03
Molecular Cell Biology
Non-coding RNAs, gene expression regulation, and cellular signaling pathways in health and disease.
04
Nanobioscience
Synthesis of Fe@Carbon dots, ZnO/Carbon dots, and Hydroxyapatite nanocomposites for antimicrobial and biomedical applications.
05
Aquatic Biology & Fisheries
Biochemical composition of Indian major carps, phylogenetics of Channa genus, and sustainable aquaculture practices.
