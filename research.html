Research

Exploring life across scales — from serotonin rhythms in the brain to nanomaterial synthesis for biomedical applications.

AREA 01

Neurophysiology

This research focus centres on understanding circadian rhythms and the biological clock mechanisms that govern daily physiological cycles. A particular emphasis is placed on serotonin dynamics — the synthesis, release, and rhythmic fluctuation of serotonin in the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN), the brain's master pacemaker.

Studies span developmental stages from postnatal life through aging, using male Wistar rat models to map how the serotonergic system evolves and how age-related changes affect circadian regulation. The role of melatonin in modulating these rhythms provides a therapeutic dimension to this research.

AREA 02

Developmental Biology

Developmental biology research investigates the fundamental molecular and cellular mechanisms that drive the growth and differentiation of animal systems. This includes neurodevelopmental processes that shape the architecture and function of the nervous system from embryonic stages onward.

The work addresses how gene regulatory networks orchestrate tissue patterning, organogenesis, and the establishment of neural circuits — with implications for understanding congenital disorders and age-related neurodegenerative changes.

AREA 03

Molecular Cell Biology

This research area explores the molecular basis of cellular function with a focus on non-coding RNA biology, particularly long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs) and their emerging roles in disease. Work on lncRNAs in hepatocellular carcinoma has established a research niche at the intersection of cancer biology and RNA biology.

The broader programme encompasses gene expression regulation, cellular signalling cascades, and epigenetic mechanisms — generating insights applicable to both fundamental biology and translational medicine.

AREA 04

Nanobioscience

The nanobioscience programme focuses on the green and sustainable synthesis of nanocomposites using plant extracts, and evaluating their biological activities. Current materials under investigation include Fe@Carbon dot nanocomposites (synthesised from Euphorbia heterophylla and Madhuca longifolia), ZnO/Carbon dots, and doped Hydroxyapatite composites.

Characterisation studies employ XRD, FTIR, SEM, and TEM. Biological screening covers antimicrobial efficacy, in vitro antioxidant activity, anti-inflammatory properties, and biocompatibility assessments for potential orthopaedic and biomedical device applications.

AREA 05

Aquatic Biology & Fisheries

Aquatic biology research addresses questions of ecological and economic relevance to the East Godavari region of Andhra Pradesh. A RUSA-funded project investigates the biochemical composition — including fatty acid profiles, protein content, and heavy metal accumulation — of Indian major carps from different aquatic ecosystems.

Parallel work examines the morphology, systematics, and molecular phylogenetics of the Channa genus in the Indian subcontinent, with comparative fatty acid profiling of wild-caught and cultured Channa striata to inform sustainable aquaculture practices.
